# Offer Pricing Funnel

## Overview

Turn demand evidence into an offer someone can buy. This skill connects niche validation to a priced package, lead magnet, landing page, and sales path.

## Source Signals

## Offer Components

| Component | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer | Specific role and budget owner |
| Pain | Close to revenue, cost, time, or risk |
| Promise | Outcome in business language |
| Mechanism | Why AI or automation solves it better now |
| Package | Setup, retainer, subscription, or audit path |
| Proof | Demo, case study, benchmark, teardown, or pilot |
| Funnel | Content, lead magnet, email, Skool, sales call, close |

## Workflow

1. Start from a `build` or `test` niche decision.
2. Choose one buyer and one painful workflow.
3. Set price anchors from competitors and current alternatives.
4. Design a low-risk entry offer and a higher-value backend.
5. Write the lead magnet and landing page promise.
6. Define qualification questions and disqualifiers.
7. Choose the first validation test.

## Common Mistakes

- Pricing from effort instead of buyer value.
- Offering "AI automation" instead of a business outcome.
- Creating too many packages before first sale.
- Forgetting disqualifiers.
- Treating a lead magnet as the offer.
