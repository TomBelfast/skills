# Community Growth System

## Overview

Build community as a trust, learning, and demand engine. Community growth should support member outcomes, surface paid problems, and convert the right members into offers without turning the group into a sales feed.

## Community Loops

| Loop | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Get new members to one useful action in 24 hours |
| Discussion | Turn content into conversation and problem discovery |
| Proof | Share wins, demos, audits, and member outcomes |
| Activation | Challenges, templates, office hours, teardown threads |
| Monetization | Audits, workshops, implementations, premium tiers |

## Workflow

1. Define member promise and paid backend.
2. Map YouTube topics to Skool discussions.
3. Create onboarding prompts that reveal member goals and business pains.
4. Run weekly activation loops with templates or audits.
5. Capture repeated pains into `community-pain-mining`.
6. Convert strong pains into lead magnets or paid offers.

## Common Mistakes

- Measuring only member count.
- Posting content without asking for member action.
- Selling too early before trust and diagnosis.
- Ignoring lurker conversion.
- Letting insights stay in chat instead of feeding the skill lab.
