# Competitor Offer Analysis

## Overview

Dissect offers to understand what buyers already pay for and where a differentiated AI app, community, or implementation offer can win.

## Offer Teardown

For each competitor, capture:

- buyer and use case
- promise and outcome
- features tied to the promise
- pricing, packages, free trial, or service model
- proof: testimonials, case studies, logos, metrics, demos
- funnel: traffic source, lead magnet, CTA, sales call, onboarding
- objections they answer
- gaps, weak claims, or underserved segments

## Competitor Types

Include more than direct SaaS:

- product competitors
- agencies and consultants
- courses and templates
- internal hiring or contractors
- spreadsheets, manual processes, and DIY workflows

## Output

| Competitor | Type | Buyer | Promise | Pricing Clue | Proof | Funnel/CTA | Gap | Polish Wedge |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Then write:

- what is commoditized
- what buyers clearly value
- what proof our offer needs
- how to position a Polish-market alternative
- decision: copy pattern, differentiate, partner, watch, or ignore

## Common Mistakes

- Comparing features without studying the offer.
- Ignoring services and manual alternatives.
- Assuming weak design means weak demand.
- Copying positioning without a wedge.
- Missing pricing clues hidden in calls, demos, reviews, affiliates, and job posts.
