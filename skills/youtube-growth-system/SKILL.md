# YouTube Growth System

## Overview

Build YouTube as the trust and demand engine for the Polish AI community. This skill adapts high-fit patterns from YouTube marketing skill packs and video marketing skills, but prioritizes conversion into Skool, email, and paid AI business apps.

## Workflow

1. Start from a paid niche or validated buyer pain.
2. Pick one content pillar and one conversion goal.
3. Create 5 video ideas with buyer intent and proof source.
4. For each selected video, produce title, hook, thumbnail brief, outline, CTA, and repurposing plan.
5. Define what metric decides whether the topic should repeat.

## Common Mistakes

- Creating videos around AI news without buyer pain.
- Optimizing views without Skool, email, or offer conversion.
- Writing scripts before the title/hook/thumbnail promise is clear.
- Treating Shorts as the whole strategy.
- Automating publishing before manual quality is proven.
