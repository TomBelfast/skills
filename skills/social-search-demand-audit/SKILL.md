# Social Search Demand Audit

## Overview

Audit whether social search can produce qualified demand. This adapts the strongest ideas from ScaleBrick's social search skills, but removes vendor CTAs and adds paid-demand gating for our AI community and business app goals.

## Source Signals

Inspired by:

- `ScaleBrick/founder-marketing-skills`: `audit`, `keywords`
- `kostja94/marketing-skills`: `reddit-posts`, `linkedin-posts`, `tiktok-captions`, `community-forum`

## Audit Dimensions

Score each from 1 to 5:

| Dimension | What 5 Means |
| --- | --- |
| Search demand | Many real problem, comparison, and tool queries |
| Paid intent | Queries point to software, services, audits, templates, pricing, or implementation |
| Competition gap | Existing content is weak, generic, or not localized |
| Content fit | The problem can be shown through short posts, carousels, comments, or videos |
| Buyer fit | The people searching can buy or influence purchase |
| Poland fit | Polish-language content or local context creates advantage |

## Workflow

1. Define the business, buyer, niche, offer, and target country.
2. Search for problem-aware, commercial, transactional, and comparison queries.
3. Check social surfaces separately: TikTok/Instagram, Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook groups.
4. Separate evidence of attention from evidence of spend.
5. Identify first 10 content tests and what each test validates.
6. Decide `strong fit`, `worth testing`, or `not a fit`.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating viral potential as buyer intent.
- Assuming TikTok/Instagram fit every market.
- Ignoring Reddit and YouTube comments as demand evidence.
- Returning hashtags instead of search phrases.
- Forgetting to test Polish-language variants.
