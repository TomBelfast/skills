# Paid Intent Research

## Overview

Prove whether money already moves around a problem. This skill separates hard payment evidence from weak interest signals.

## Hard Signals

Look for evidence such as:

- active SaaS tools with pricing pages
- agencies, consultants, freelancers, or productized services
- paid ads, sponsored listings, affiliate programs, or marketplace demand
- job posts, contractors, RFPs, tenders, or implementation partners
- communities, courses, templates, audits, or retainers with clear pricing
- case studies where a buyer paid to solve the problem

## Weak Signals

Do not count these as payment proof by themselves:

- high search volume
- social media hype
- free tools
- blog posts
- generic "AI for X" trend pieces
- complaints without budget owner or buying path

## Research Steps

1. Name the economic buyer and budget owner.
2. List hard paid-intent signals with source links when available.
3. List weak signals separately.
4. Estimate how the buyer currently solves the problem.
5. Identify price anchors and minimum viable paid offer.
6. Decide whether to continue, test, or reject.

## Output

Use this structure:

| Signal | Source/Example | Buyer | Price/Budget Clue | Strength 1-5 | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Then write:

- hard-signal count
- weak-signal count
- buyer and budget owner
- confidence: low, medium, or high
- decision: `continue`, `test`, or `reject`

## Decision Rule

Do not recommend continuing unless there are at least two hard signals or one very strong signal tied to a clear budget owner.
